About me

I’m Aitesam, an electrical engineer specializing in embedded systems, hardware design, and product prototyping. I develop electronics and firmware for IoT and industrial applications, with experience taking commercial products from design and prototyping through manufacturing and component sourcing.

I’m also interested in computer architecture, RISC-V systems-on-chip, and RTL design for FPGAs and ASICs. I enjoy learning new design techniques and exploring how hardware and software work together.

I founded Helium Labs, a small engineering consultancy and solutions team. I share projects on GitHub and occasionally document my PCB design process on YouTube.
